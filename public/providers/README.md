# Provider marks

These marks appear only beside actions that open the named provider. They do
not imply sponsorship or endorsement.

- `google.svg` is the unmodified, pre-approved light square icon from Google's
  [Sign in with Google asset bundle](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines).
- `google-drive.png` is the unmodified
  [Google Drive product logo](https://developers.google.com/workspace/drive/api/guides/branding).
  Google Drive is a trademark of Google LLC. Use of this trademark is subject
  to Google Permissions.
- `microsoft.svg` is Microsoft's unmodified 21-pixel symbol from the
  [Microsoft identity-platform branding guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/entra/identity-platform/howto-add-branding-in-apps).
- `dropbox.png` is the unmodified glyph published by the
  [Dropbox Brand Guidelines](https://brand.dropbox.com/logo) and is used under
  the functional-use rules in the
  [Dropbox Developer Branding Guide](https://www.dropbox.com/developers/reference/branding-guide).
- `github-dark-on-light.svg` and `github-light-on-dark.svg` are the unmodified
  Invertocat variants from GitHub's official
  [logo download](https://brand.github.com/foundations/logo).

Google, Microsoft, OneDrive, and Dropbox marks remain the property of their
respective owners.
